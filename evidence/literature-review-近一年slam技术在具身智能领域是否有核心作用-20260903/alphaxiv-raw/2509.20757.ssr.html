Abstract

Visual SLAM is a cornerstone technique in robotics, autonomous driving and extended reality (XR), yet classical systems often struggle with low-texture environments, scale ambiguity, and degraded performance under challenging visual conditions. Recent advancements in feed-forward neural network-based pointmap regression have demonstrated the potential to recover high-fidelity 3D scene geometry directly from images, leveraging learned spatial priors to overcome limitations of traditional multi-view geometry methods. However, the widely validated advantages of probabilistic multi-sensor information fusion are often discarded in these pipelines. In this work, we propose MASt3R-Fusion,a multi-sensor-assisted visual SLAM framework that tightly integrates feed-forward pointmap regression with complementary sensor information, including inertial measurements and GNSS data. The system introduces Sim(3)-based visualalignment constraints (in the Hessian form) into a universal metric-scale SE(3) factor graph for effective information fusion. A hierarchical factor graph design is developed, which allows both real-time sliding-window optimization and global optimization with aggressive loop closures, enabling real-time pose tracking, metric-scale structure perception and globally consistent mapping. We evaluate our approach on both public benchmarks and self-collected datasets, demonstrating substantial improvements in accuracy and robustness over existing visual-centered multi-sensor SLAM systems. The code will be released open-source to support reproducibility and further research (this https URL).

AI Overview

Introduction

Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) represents a fundamental challenge in robotics, autonomous driving, and extended reality applications. While traditional visual SLAM systems have achieved remarkable success, they often struggle with inherent limitations such as scale ambiguity, poor performance in low-texture environments, and degraded accuracy under challenging visual conditions. Recent advances in deep learning have introduced feed-forward neural network-based pointmap regression models that can directly recover high-fidelity 3D scene geometry from images by leveraging learned spatial priors. However, these learning-based approaches still face challenges in maintaining consistent metric scale over long sequences and can falter in visually deprived environments.

MASt3R-Fusion addresses this critical gap by proposing a novel framework that tightly integrates the powerful capabilities of feed-forward visual models with complementary information from Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs) and Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS). The system bridges the scale-ambiguous Sim(3) space of visual models with the metric-scale SE(3) information provided by IMUs and GNSS, creating a comprehensive multi-sensor SLAM solution.

Core Technical Approach

Feed-Forward Visual Processing

The foundation of MASt3R-Fusion lies in its use of MASt3R, a two-view feed-forward model that processes image pairs to directly regress 2D-to-3D pointmaps and pixel-level descriptor maps. Unlike traditional visual SLAM systems that rely on sparse feature extraction and multi-view geometry, this approach leverages learned 3D spatial priors to provide dense, high-fidelity geometric information.

The feed-forward model encodes image pairs into feature tokens and decodes them to produce pointmaps (Xiji,Xijj)(X_{ij}^i, X_{ij}^j)(Xiji​,Xijj​) and descriptor maps (Diji,Dijj)(D_{ij}^i, D_{ij}^j)(Diji​,Dijj​) in a common reference frame. This enables dense data association through a two-step process: ray proximity optimization followed by feature-based refinement using descriptor maps and sub-pixel upsampling.

Sim(3)-Based Visual Constraints

A key innovation in MASt3R-Fusion is its formulation of visual constraints through pointmap alignment rather than traditional bundle adjustment. Camera poses are represented in the Sim(3) group, which includes rotation, translation, and scale components:

Si=[siRisiti01]S_i = \begin{bmatrix} s_i R_i & s_i t_i \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}Si​=[si​Ri​0​si​ti​1​]

Visual residuals are formulated to align pointmaps from co-visible frames, incorporating both reprojection errors and depth consistency components. This approach directly estimates inter-frame relative Sim(3) transformations while avoiding the complexity of parameterizing numerous individual point depths.

Isomorphic Group Transformation

The critical challenge of integrating Sim(3)-based visual constraints with metric-scale SE(3) states from IMU and GNSS is solved through an isomorphic group transformation. The system factorizes Sim(3) into an SE(3) transformation and scalar scaling, allowing consistent representation of states as (T,s)∈SE(3)×R(T, s) \in SE(3) \times \mathbb{R}(T,s)∈SE(3)×R.

A linear transformation matrix Λ\LambdaΛ is derived to bridge the Lie algebras of Sim(3) and SE(3) × R:

⊞sim(3)(δξ)=Λ⊞SE(3)×R(δχ)\boxplus_{\text{sim(3)}}(\delta \xi) = \Lambda \boxplus_{SE(3) \times \mathbb{R}}(\delta \chi)⊞sim(3)​(δξ)=Λ⊞SE(3)×R​(δχ)

This transformation enables seamless fusion of visual constraints with IMU and GNSS measurements within a unified factor graph framework.

Hierarchical Architecture

Real-Time SLAM Stage

The real-time component operates as a sliding-window optimization that integrates visual constraints with IMU data. Keyframe states are defined as:

Xi=(Ti,si,vi,bi)X_i = (T_i, s_i, v_i, b_i)Xi​=(Ti​,si​,vi​,bi​)

where TiT_iTi​ represents the pose, sis_isi​ the scale, viv_ivi​ the velocity, and bib_ibi​ the IMU bias.

The system maintains computational efficiency through GPU acceleration for visual computations while performing sliding-window optimization in double precision for numerical stability. Visual Hessian information computed on GPU is transformed using the isomorphic group transformation and integrated with IMU factors to form the complete factor graph.

Global Optimization Stage

The global stage processes logged visual-inertial information with loop closures and GNSS data for globally consistent mapping. Key components include:

Loop Closure Detection: Image feature tokens from the feed-forward encoder are used for image retrieval, generating loop closure candidates. A novel geometry-based filtering mechanism estimates VIO odometry uncertainty and applies co-visibility criteria to reduce false positives.

GNSS Integration: GNSS measurements are incorporated through residuals between measured positions and estimated camera poses, accounting for IMU-GNSS lever arm and initial alignment:

rg=t~g,in−(Rnw)T(Ritbc+ti−twn)r_g = \tilde{t}_{g,i}^n - (R_n^w)^T (R_i t_{bc} + t_i - t_{wn})rg​=t~g,in​−(Rnw​)T(Ri​tbc​+ti​−twn​)

Two-Step Optimization: The global optimization employs a robust two-step strategy. Initial optimization uses relative pose constraints with Cauchy kernel robustification, followed by refined optimization incorporating full Hessian-form visual constraints for inlier loop closures.

Experimental Validation

Performance on Public Benchmarks

The system demonstrates superior performance across multiple datasets. On KITTI-360, MASt3R-Fusion achieves 43.0% lower Relative Translation Error compared to DM-VIO and 17.7% lower than DBA-Fusion for real-time visual-inertial odometry. With loop closure activated, the system achieves an impressive 0.05% ATE/length ratio, demonstrating exceptional global consistency.

Figure 3: KITTI-360 trajectory results showing (a) odometry performance without loop closure and (b) global SLAM performance with loop closure, demonstrating superior accuracy compared to existing methods.

On the challenging SubT-MRS cave dataset, MASt3R-Fusion consistently outperforms traditional methods, validating its robustness in unconventional environments where classical feature-based approaches often fail.

Dense 3D Perception Capabilities

Figure 4: Comparison of real-time 3D spatial perception capabilities showing MASt3R-Fusion's ability to provide dense, metric-scale 3D structure compared to other approaches.

The system provides real-time, dense 3D spatial perception with metric scale awareness. Compared to traditional approaches that yield sparse point clouds or dedicated monocular depth models that can have scale inconsistencies, MASt3R-Fusion maintains overall 3D structure coherence through its binocular geometry and learned spatial priors.

Urban Environment Performance

Testing on challenging urban datasets demonstrates the system's practical applicability. In environments with frequent GNSS outages and degraded conditions, MASt3R-Fusion maintains decimeter-level accuracy through comprehensive multi-sensor fusion and robust optimization strategies.

Technical Contributions and Significance

MASt3R-Fusion makes several key technical contributions to the SLAM field:

  1. Novel Integration Framework: The first system to tightly couple feed-forward pointmap regression models with multi-sensor information through a principled Sim(3) to SE(3) transformation.

  2. Robust Visual Constraints: Reformulation of visual SLAM as pointmap alignment rather than traditional bundle adjustment, leveraging learned 3D priors for enhanced robustness.

  3. Comprehensive Multi-Sensor Fusion: Unified factor graph framework incorporating visual, inertial, and GNSS measurements with full information preservation.

  4. Geometry-Based Loop Filtering: Novel probabilistic filtering mechanism for loop closure candidates based on VIO uncertainty estimation and co-visibility analysis.

The system represents a significant advance in bridging learning-based visual perception with established probabilistic sensor fusion techniques. By successfully integrating the complementary strengths of feed-forward visual models (high-fidelity geometry, aggressive data association) with multi-sensor information (metric scale, drift reduction, global consistency), MASt3R-Fusion achieves state-of-the-art performance across diverse challenging scenarios.

The comprehensive experimental validation demonstrates substantial improvements in accuracy, robustness, and functionality compared to existing visual-centered multi-sensor SLAM systems, positioning MASt3R-Fusion as a highly capable solution for real-world autonomous navigation applications.
